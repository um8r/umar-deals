Buying tips

  • Lawn and cotton are cool for summer; khaddar, dhanak and linen are warmer for winter.
  • For unstitched suits, check the number of pieces: 3-piece includes shirt, trouser and dupatta.
  • Hand wash or gentle wash in cold water to keep colours bright.
