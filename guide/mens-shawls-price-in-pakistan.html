Buying tips

  • Shawl length is often given in gazz (yards): 2.5 gazz suits most men, while 2.75 or 3 gazz gives a fuller wrap for taller men.
  • Dark colours like black, brown and grey are easiest to match with shalwar kameez.
  • Hand wash gently in cold water or dry clean, and dry in shade.
